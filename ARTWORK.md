## Mascot prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an ORIGINAL anime-style mascot for Hive, a dark charcoal and amber developer product. A confident adult bee-themed scout warrior with warm brown skin, tousled dark curls with one amber highlight, small antennae, smoked amber goggles pushed up, black and honey-yellow segmented flight armor over a dark bodysuit, two translucent golden bee wings opened asymmetrically, amber-black striped scarf and small hex-shaped utility satchel. Natural athletic proportions, human face with warm, playful confidence. Three-quarter full figure, floating lightly, posed facing slightly left toward website copy. Premium contemporary anime key art with controlled cel shading, clean silhouette and painterly detail, luminous honey rim light. Entire head, wings, hands and boots visible, generous safe margin. No weapon, no text, no logo, no environment, clean alpha matte TRANSPARENT background.

## Portrait atlas prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Make ONE perfectly aligned 2x2 character portrait atlas, square canvas with equal quadrants, in original high-end bee-themed anime concept art for Hive. Exactly four distinct adult humanoid bee characters, one shoulder-up portrait per quadrant, same scale, each centered at its quadrant center with the complete face, hair and antennae within its quadrant; no overlapping, NO gutters, NO lines, NO lettering or symbols. Upper-left a brown-skinned dark curly-haired bee scout with amber goggles, warm bronze scout field. Upper-right a tan short-haired bee engineer with gold workwear and a warm green-toned field. Lower-left a silver-haired bee designer with pale lilac-tinted wing fragments and muted violet backdrop. Lower-right a dark-skinned bee reviewer with a navy visor at the forehead and a subdued cool steel field. Each distinct silhouette, grounded likeness, lively expressions, crisp clean linework, black-and-honey costume accents and small antennae. Matte dark richly colored backgrounds in each quadrant and amber rim light. Each square is an intact portrait that can be cropped neatly at the exact horizontal and vertical midpoint. No typography, no UI, no weapons, no logos.
